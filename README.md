# My Favorite Series: *Uncanny Counter*

**Uncanny Counter** is a *Korean fantasy-action* series that follows a group of demon hunters called *Counters*. They disguise themselves as employees at a noodle shop while secretly fighting evil spirits that escape from the afterlife.

---

## 🎬 Main Characters
1. **So Mun** – The youngest Counter with a tragic past and strong determination.  
2. **Ga Mo-tak** – A former police officer known for his strength and bravery.  
3. **Do Ha-na** – Can sense evil spirits from a distance and read memories.  
4. **Chu Mae-ok** – The healer and mother figure of the group.  

---

## 🌟 Why I Like It
> “Uncanny Counter” is inspiring because it shows teamwork, courage, and fighting for justice even when things get hard.

- Action-packed scenes  
- Emotional storytelling  
- Strong character development  
- Cool supernatural elements
